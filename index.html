About Me

self portrait

Hi, I'm Louisa — 16 years old and so glad you found your way here. This little corner of the internet is a collection of everything that makes my heart feel full.

I was born on November 23rd, which I think perfectly explains why I love cozy things — blankets, warm drinks, and soft afternoons.

Speaking of warm drinks, I am absolutely obsessed with matcha. Yes, I know — not everyone gets it. People always have opinions about it, but honestly? It's earthy, it's calming, and I will never stop. The matcha girlies understand.

My aesthetic lives somewhere between a sunny spring morning and a bedroom lit only by fairy lights. I collect Sonny Angels, press flowers, and tie ribbons on anything that will let me.

Current loves:

  • Matcha (judge me, I dare you)
  • Sonny Angel figurines
  • Pressed flowers
  • Overcast November afternoons
  • Ribbons tied on everything
