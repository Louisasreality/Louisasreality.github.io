About Me

self portrait

Hi, my name is Louisa. I am 16 years old and a current sophomore at Brooke High School in Massachusetts. So glad you found your way here.

In my free time I love watching anime - my favorites right now are Bleach, Nana, and Vinland Saga. All three hit completely different emotions and I think that says a lot about me.

I am a big fan of strawberry matcha - it is the perfect drink and I will not be taking questions. I also love drawing whenever inspiration strikes.

One of my biggest passions is learning about neuroscience and other STEM fields. The brain is genuinely one of the most fascinating things I have ever studied, and I hope to major in a STEM field when I get to college.
